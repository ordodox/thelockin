# The Lock-In

The doors are bolted, the glasses are stacked, and the landlord has
chalked a number on the slate. That number is your tab. The only legal
tender after hours is the game the pub was built around: a long slate
table, a pocketful of pucks, and a flick.

Pull back, let go. The puck slides, knocks what it knocks, and stops
where physics says it stops. The beds at the far end pay 1, 2 and 3 —
but a bed only has **so many seats**, and the house's own coins were on
the slate before you walked in. A squatter in a bed is a seat you can't
score. Knock it out and the seat opens back up. Eight pucks a night;
settle the tab or the house keeps the table.

Every night is dealt from a seed: the same seed lays the same slate,
squatters and all, on any device. Which also means the *challenge link*
is the whole multiplayer — send a friend your night and they play your
exact table, no server, no account, just the same physics reaching the
same verdict.

And once a day there's the **house round**: everyone locked in on the
same calendar day plays the same table. The group chat is the
leaderboard.

**To play:** open it in your browser —
[get locked in](https://ordodox.github.io/thelockin/) — or grab
`index.html` and open it straight from disk. No install, works with the
internet off. Your night survives a closed tab: the flicks you've made
are the save, and the table re-plays them to the puck when you return.
